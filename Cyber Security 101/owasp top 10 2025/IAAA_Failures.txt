##IAAA
Basically I triple A is a simple way to think about how user and their actions are verified on the application Like each item plays a crucial role and it isn't possible to skip a level that means if a previous item isn't being performed you cannot perform the later times the four items are:
Identity the IT which is a unique account that represents a person's or services
Authentication proving that ident like password on
Authorization which is what that identity is allowed to do
Accountability record and alert on who did what And from where
